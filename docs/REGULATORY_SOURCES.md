# Regulatory Source Register

This register separates official source portals from claims that still need a
case-specific legal review. The application must not treat a model-generated
citation as verified merely because it names an IRDAI circular.

## Verified official portals

- [IRDAI](https://irdai.gov.in/) - regulator website, policyholder information,
  circulars, guidelines, and document archive.
- [Bima Bharosa](https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/) - IRDAI complaint and
  grievance portal.
- [Council for Insurance Ombudsmen](https://cioins.co.in/) - official
  Ombudsman process, eligibility, complaint filing, jurisdiction, and archives.

The CIO website currently states that a complainant should first approach the
insurer or broker, wait one month for a response or dissatisfaction with the
response, file within one year of rejection or expiry of that month, and seek
compensation not exceeding Rs. 50 lakh. These conditions should still be checked
against the current rules when a letter is prepared.

## Citation policy

Every regulatory claim used in a generated letter should contain:

1. The exact instrument title.
2. Regulation, circular, rule, or section number where available.
3. Publication date or effective date.
4. A URL to the official IRDAI or CIO document.
5. The document version checked by the reviewer.

## Claims requiring manual verification

The existing knowledge base contains named circular identifiers, timelines,
interest calculations, standard-procedure counts, and narrative Ombudsman
precedents. These should not be presented as authoritative until each entry is
matched to an official document or an identifiable CIO order. In particular,
review the entries for:

- cashless and reimbursement timelines;
- the 8-year moratorium rule and exceptions;
- the 541-procedure list;
- the 2022 mental-health coverage references;
- modern-treatment and cashless-everywhere circular identifiers;
- the 2% above bank-rate interest claim;
- all insurer-specific patterns;
- all Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Pune, and Kolkata
  Ombudsman summaries.

Until that review is complete, generated output should describe these as
possible leads for verification, not final legal conclusions.
