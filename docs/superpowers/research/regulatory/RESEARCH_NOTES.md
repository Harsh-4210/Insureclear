# Regulatory source research, 2026-11-05

Three official artifacts were downloaded directly from IRDAI and Gazette of
India endpoints and their PDF signatures, extracted page counts and SHA-256
hashes were checked. retrieval-ledger.json records the exact URLs and hashes.
These are proposed registry inputs; they have not yet been wired into runtime.

The official English-only policyholder circular attachment is 91,401,689 bytes.
The combined official version is smaller and was used within the research size
limit. No third-party mirror was substituted. Source artifact integrity is not
proof of a rule's present legal applicability or of a model interpretation.
The proposed first-phase registry keeps case-specific legal applications under
human review.

The paired pages.json files contain extracted page text and retain page numbers.
Images are inspection artifacts, not an edited or republished circular. All
material here is public regulatory material; no claim documents are included.
