POLICY TERMS AND CONDITIONS
========================================================================

SECTION 1: SCOPE OF COVERAGE

1.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation: Covered. Minimum 24 hours hospitalisation required unless the procedure is listed under Day Care Procedures (Annexure A).

1.2 Pre-Hospitalisation: Expenses incurred 30 days prior to hospitalisation are covered, related to the same ailment.

1.3 Post-Hospitalisation: Expenses incurred 60 days after discharge are covered, related to the same ailment.

1.4 Day Care Procedures: 541 listed procedures as per IRDAI standardised list. No 24-hour hospitalisation requirement for listed procedures.

1.5 Ambulance Charges: Road ambulance covered up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation.

1.6 AYUSH Treatment: Covered in government-recognised hospitals.

1.7 Organ Donor Expenses: Covered up to Sum Insured.

========================================================================

SECTION 2: SUM INSURED AND SUB-LIMITS

2.1 Total Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 per family per policy year.

2.2 Room Rent: Limited to ₹4,000 per day or 1% of Sum Insured, whichever is lower. If actual room rent exceeds this limit, proportionate deduction shall apply to all associated medical expenses including surgeon fees, anaesthetist charges, and consumables.

2.3 ICU Charges: Limited to ₹8,000 per day or 2% of Sum Insured, whichever is lower.

2.4 Disease-Specific Sub-Limits:
  (a) Cataract: ₹20,000 per eye
  (b) Knee Replacement: ₹1,50,000 per knee
  (c) Hernia Surgery: ₹50,000
  (d) Kidney Stones / Lithotripsy: ₹40,000

2.5 Co-Payment: No co-payment applicable for members below 60 years of age. 20% co-payment for members 60 years and above.

========================================================================

SECTION 3: NETWORK HOSPITALS

3.1 Cashless facility available at network hospitals listed on www.starhealth.in/network-hospitals.

3.2 Cashless pre-authorisation must be requested at least 48 hours before planned admission.

3.3 Emergency admissions: Pre-authorisation within 24 hours of admission.

3.4 Reimbursement available at any hospital (network or non-network).

========================================================================

SECTION 4: WAITING PERIODS AND EXCLUSIONS

4.1 Waiting Periods:
  (a) Initial Waiting Period: No claims during first 30 days of policy inception, except for accidental hospitalisation.
  (b) Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period: 4 years from the date of first policy issuance. Conditions existing before purchasing this policy are not covered during this period.
  (c) Specific Disease Waiting Period: 2 years from date of first policy issuance for the following conditions:
    - Cataract and associated procedures
    - Joint replacement surgery
    - Benign ENT disorders
    - Hernia of all types
    - Fistula / piles / sinuses
    - Kidney / ureter / bladder stones
    - Internal tumours / cysts / polyps (benign)
    - Uterine fibroid / myoma / endometriosis
    - Hysterectomy for menorrhagia
    - Surgery for prolapsed intervertebral disc

4.2 Permanent Exclusions:
  (a) Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment (unless required for reconstruction after an accident)
  (b) Dental treatment (unless requiring hospitalisation and due to accident)
  (c) Vision correction including spectacles, contact lenses, LASIK
  (d) Sterility, infertility, and assisted reproduction
  (e) Self-inflicted injuries
  (f) War, invasion, nuclear events
  (g) Hazardous or adventure sports injuries
  (h) Obesity treatment and bariatric surgery (unless BMI > 40 with comorbidities)
  (i) Change of sex / gender reassignment (unless medically certified)
  (j) External congenital anomalies

4.3 What Cannot Be Excluded (Mandatory Coverage per IRDAI):
  (a) Mental illness — covered at par with physical illness
  (b) HIV/AIDS treatment
  (c) Day care procedures on IRDAI's 541-procedure list
  (d) Road ambulance
  (e) AYUSH treatment in recognised hospitals
  (f) Modern treatment methods as listed by IRDAI

SECTION 6: CLAIM PROCEDURE

6.1 Cashless Claims: Request pre-authorisation through TPA (Medi Assist). TPA must respond within 1 hour.

6.2 Reimbursement Claims: Submit original bills, discharge summary, prescription, and investigation reports within 15 days of discharge.

6.3 The insurer shall settle or reject the claim within 30 days of receiving complete documentation.

6.4 If documentation is deficient, the insurer must notify the claimant within 15 days of receiving the claim.

6.5 Any delay beyond 30 days: Interest payable at bank rate + 2%.
